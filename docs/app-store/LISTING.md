## Description (4000)

<!-- listing:description -->
SteelBuild Pro is project control for steel fabricators and erectors. The detailing office, the shop and the field crew all work from the same drawings, RFIs, submittals and schedule.

The iPhone and iPad app is for members of a SteelBuild Pro company workspace. Your workspace admin invites you by email.

DRAWINGS AND DOCUMENTS
• Drawing sets, revisions and sign-offs in one register
• Document control and GC documents
• Open and share drawings and documents from the jobsite

RFIS AND SUBMITTALS
• Track RFIs and who has the ball, from question to answer
• Keep each submittal tied to the drawing set it releases

FABRICATION
• Work packages and a piece register
• Fab release checks for approved drawings, open RFIs and holds before steel goes to the shop
• Production status, procurement and deliveries

SCHEDULE AND FIELD
• Project schedule, with a task list built for phones
• Daily logs, inspections, safety, quality control and punch lists
• Take jobsite photos with the camera and attach them to daily logs and punch list items

COST AND CLOSEOUT
• Budget control, change orders, schedule of values and pay applications
• Backcharge tracking, expenses, and labor, equipment and materials
• Job status reports you can share from your phone

YOUR ACCOUNT
You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Profile.
<!-- /listing:description -->
